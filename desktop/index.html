Rainbow Apocalypse can rapidly cycle colors. Impact Frame can create repeated high-contrast flashes. These effects may cause discomfort and may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

Neither effect will turn on until you explicitly agree. You can leave them disabled and continue using the rest of the site.
